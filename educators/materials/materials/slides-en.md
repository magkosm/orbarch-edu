### Slide 2 — What is a space station?
- A spacecraft that stays in orbit so crews can **live and work** there for weeks to months — a home and workplace in orbit
> Not a brief visit; crews live with the design every hour. First station crew (Salyut 1, 1971): 24 days; ISS crews: ~6 months.
